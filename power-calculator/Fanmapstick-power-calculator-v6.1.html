Power and processing use

Now, they are by no means perfect but below is a small collection of data analysis tools to help you see my point. Feel free to read it and play with it to see what is what but I'll summarize the intent behind it.

Going based off what data I could collect from various research papers and the profiling tool I used on this web toy I put this data together which shows that yes AI images can be cheaper for smaller images with the most efficient models but in most cases the processing and power use cost per image is much higher than with my app.

80

In one 30-minute session you can export many maps. The session cost is paid once and each extra map only adds its export. Each map also counts as one more AI image, so both bars grow with this number. Drag the box up or down to change it, or click it and type. The chart axis never changes with the slider: linear runs 0 to 500 Wh, logarithmic runs 0.001 to 1000 Wh. A bar that goes past the end is cut off with an arrow and its real value is written beside it.

This page is the Power and processing use section of BEVM (Bird's Eye View Map), part of Fanmapstick. It runs entirely in your browser and contacts nothing.
